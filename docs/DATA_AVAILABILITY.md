# Data availability and privacy

Patient-level development and validation datasets are not included in this public repository because of privacy, informed-consent, and institutional-governance restrictions.

The public archive contains:
- the locked RF model object;
- prediction and Shiny source code;
- publication-lock feature-selection code;
- a variable dictionary and model specification;
- synthetic example input/output;
- software/session information;
- reproducibility and concordance scripts.

De-identified participant data may be considered by the corresponding authors and participating institutions upon reasonable request and applicable approval, consistent with the manuscript's data-availability statement.

No real patient records should be committed to this repository.
